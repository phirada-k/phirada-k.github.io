Comotion 2024 Title Sequence

Collaborate with SCAD's MomeLove. I created a full CG shot for title sequence in a theme of life. The process includes reporting to design lead and present iterations of the shots. The styleframe and sketches were given with some creative liberty that I can utilize Houdini's procedural aspect. Most shot used randomize attribute but still controlable. Render different passes with Redshift and composite in Nuke.
